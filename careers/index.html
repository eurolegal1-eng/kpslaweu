Careers & Opportunities

Build Your Future With Us

We welcome talented, motivated and curious people who want to grow with a forward-looking legal practice.

Kim Partners Solicitors welcomes expressions of interest from people committed to professional excellence, client service, integrity and continuous learning.

Who We Would Like to Hear From

  • Qualified solicitors and legal consultants
  • Paralegals and legal assistants
  • Law graduates and aspiring solicitors
  • Administrative and client support professionals
  • Business development, marketing and LegalTech professionals

Depending on operational requirements, opportunities may be full-time, part-time, flexible or project-based. Training contracts, qualifying work experience and internships are not guaranteed and are subject to separate arrangements.

Our Areas of Interest

Corporate and commercial law, property, employment, immigration, disputes, international business and technology-enabled legal services.

Express Your Interest

Send a CV and a short introduction outlining your interests, relevant experience and preferred working arrangements.

Email Your Application

Please do not include identity documents, sensitive personal data or confidential client information in an initial email. Applications are considered as opportunities arise; an application does not guarantee an interview or an offer. Applicant information will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection law. A recruitment privacy notice and retention schedule should be approved before enabling an online application upload form.

Equal Opportunities

We welcome applications from candidates of diverse backgrounds and assess candidates against the requirements of each opportunity.
